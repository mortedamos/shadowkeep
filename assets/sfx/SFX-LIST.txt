HOW THIS WORKS
--------------
Every sound in the game is, for now, SYNTHESIZED by the game itself (the
beeps, whooshes and bells you hear).  Each one can be replaced by a real
recording, with no code changes:

  1. Find a sound that fits (see "WHERE TO LOOK" at the bottom).
  2. Save it in THIS folder (assets/sfx/) using the FILE NAME below.
     .mp3, .ogg or .wav all work: "jump.mp3", "jump.ogg" or "jump.wav".
  3. Reload the game.  That's all.  A sound with no file keeps its built-in
     synthesized version, so you can add them one at a time, in any order.

VARIANTS: for sounds that repeat a lot (footsteps, sword swings, hits),
a few different recordings sound much better than one.  Add the extra ones
with _2, _3, ... on the end of the name (step.wav, step_2.wav, step_3.wav).
The game picks one at random each time and bends its pitch up or down by up to 7% (every file, even a single one, so repeats never sound identical).

PUBLISHING: a plain web host (GitHub Pages) cannot list a folder, so before
you publish run   node tools/sfx-manifest.mjs   (it writes assets/sfx/files.json).
Playing locally with  node server.js  needs nothing.

TIPS FOR EACH FILE
 * Trim the silence off the start - the sound must begin the instant it plays.
 * Keep them SHORT (the length is given below); no long reverb tails on the
   sounds that repeat.
 * Normalise them to about the same loudness (peak around -3 dB).  The game
   has a SOUND volume slider, and plays every file at about 90% of its level.
 * Mono is fine and keeps the files small.
 * Write down where each one came from and its licence (CREDITS.md and
   credits.txt need a line for it).  Prefer CC0 / public domain.

(The old "record_scratch.mp3" is already here: it is the record-scratch gag
in the disco room, and is separate from this list.)


THE LIST
--------
Order: the ones you will hear MOST come first - they make the biggest
difference.  "length" is a good length to look for.

---------- 1. HEARD CONSTANTLY (do these first) ----------

step         length 0.05-0.15 s     The hero's (and every creature's) footfall.  Soft stone / dirt step.
             Find 3-4 variants: step.wav step_2.wav step_3.wav step_4.wav   (very quiet and dry)
slash        length 0.2-0.4 s       A sword swing: a quick airy WHOOSH, no impact.  Hero and enemies both use it.
             Variants: slash.wav slash_2.wav slash_3.wav
hit          length 0.15-0.3 s      A blade or blow LANDING on an enemy: a meaty thwack / slice-into-flesh.
             Variants: hit.wav hit_2.wav hit_3.wav
hurt         length 0.3-0.6 s       The hero is wounded: a grunt of pain, or a heavy body-blow thud.
jump         length 0.1-0.25 s      The hero (or a boss) leaps: a light cloth-and-boot push-off.
land         length 0.1-0.2 s       Landing after a jump: a soft thud of boots on stone.
kill         length 0.3-0.7 s       An enemy dies: a short burst / crumble / ghostly gasp.
coin         length 0.15-0.4 s      Gold picked up: a bright little coin clink or two.
pickup       length 0.1-0.3 s       A heart or mana drop is picked up: a small soft chime.
click        length 0.03-0.08 s     Menu cursor moves: a tiny tick.
select       length 0.08-0.2 s      Menu choice confirmed: a short bright blip or wooden knock.
back         length 0.08-0.2 s      Menu cancelled: a short lower blip.

---------- 2. COMBAT AND MAGIC ----------

djump        length 0.2-0.4 s       The DOUBLE jump: a second airy flutter - like wings or a puff of wind.
dash         length 0.2-0.4 s       The hero's dash: a fast rushing swoosh.
block        length 0.1-0.25 s      A blow bounces off (armour or a shield): a bright metallic CLANG.
fire         length 0.4-0.9 s       A flame bursts or burns: a rushing fire WHOOSH / crackle.
fireball     length 0.3-0.7 s       A fireball is launched: a fiery whoosh with a bit of pitch rise.
cast         length 0.3-0.7 s       Any spell being cast / an enemy sorcerer conjuring: a rising magical shimmer.
enchant      length 0.5-1.0 s       The hero's sword takes an element (fire / ice / sun): sparkling magical shing.
freeze       length 0.3-0.7 s       Ice: a glassy crystalline crackle-chime.
thunder      length 1.5-3.0 s       The thunder spell and the storms: a big lightning crack rolling into thunder.
crack        length 0.3-0.7 s       A sharp CRACK: lightning strike, glass breaking, a wall splitting (also the Mirror).
boom         length 0.4-1.0 s       A heavy impact or blast: boss slams, explosions, a crash.  Deep and punchy.
break        length 0.3-0.7 s       Something smashes: a barrel, a clay pot, a statue, a cracked wall (wood / stone / pottery).
bone         length 0.05-0.2 s      A skeleton's bones clatter: a dry rattle or a bone clack.
flap         length 0.1-0.3 s       Wings beat (bats, birds, the Count's mist): one soft flutter.
shriek       length 0.5-1.2 s       A piercing screech: ghosts, bats, the Seraph.  High and eerie.
ghost        length 0.5-1.5 s       A ghostly moan or whisper: low, airy, hollow.
roar         length 0.8-1.8 s       A boss's roar: a deep monstrous growl / bellow.
tell         length 0.15-0.35 s     The WARNING just before a boss attack: a sharp two-note alert tick (it is a gameplay
                                    signal, so keep it clear and short).
steam        length 0.3-0.8 s       A burst of steam: a hiss (the Clockwork Dungeon).
gear         length 0.05-0.2 s      A cog or mechanism clunk: a small metal tick or clank (Clockwork Dungeon).

---------- 3. WORLD, ITEMS AND FANFARES ----------

heal         length 0.5-1.0 s       A potion or heart restores health: a warm rising chime / sparkle.
mana         length 0.4-0.8 s       Mana restored: a cool watery shimmer.
item         length 0.8-1.5 s       A special item is picked up (a key, a relic): a bright magical jingle.
levelup      length 0.5-1.2 s       The hero gains a level: a short triumphant arpeggio / fanfare.
ability      length 1.5-3.0 s       A great power is won (double jump, dash, ember, thunder...): a big rising, glowing swell.
door         length 0.6-1.2 s       A heavy door opens, or you pass through one: wood or stone grinding, creaking.
seal         length 0.5-1.0 s       A boss room's doors SEAL shut behind you: a grinding slam plus a magical hum.
unseal       length 0.5-1.0 s       The seals are lifted when a boss falls: a release - the hum fading, the doors unlocking.
save         length 1.5-3.0 s       The sanctuary / altar saves your game: soft bells, calm and holy.
toll         length 2.5-4.5 s       A single deep church BELL tolling (the opening scene and story beats).  Low and resonant;
                                    let it ring out.
gameover     length 1.5-3.0 s       The hero dies: a sinking, mournful drone or a low descending toll.


NOT USED RIGHT NOW (ignore these): candle, zap, shield.  They exist in the
game but nothing plays them at the moment.


WHERE TO LOOK
-------------
 * freesound.org           (huge; filter the licence to "Creative Commons 0" and you can use anything without credit)
 * kenney.nl/assets        (CC0 sound packs: "Impact Sounds", "RPG Audio", "Interface Sounds", "Digital Audio")
 * opengameart.org         (search "sword", "footsteps", "spell"; read the licence on each page)
 * sonniss.com GDC bundles (free professional sound libraries, royalty-free for games)
 * pixabay.com/sound-effects (free; credit not required)
Search words that work well:  "sword whoosh", "sword hit flesh", "stone footstep", "wooden door creak",
"church bell toll", "fire whoosh", "ice crystal", "lightning thunder crack", "magic spell cast",
"ghost moan", "monster roar", "bone rattle", "steam hiss", "gear clank", "coin pickup".
